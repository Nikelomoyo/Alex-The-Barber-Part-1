## Project Overview

Alex the Barber is a fictional professional barbershop website developed as part of the Website Project. The website is designed to provide customers with information about the organisation, its services, pricing, locations and contact details.

The website aims to establish a professional online presence for Alex the Barber and make it easier for customers to access information and submit service enquiries.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

The main goals and objectives of the website are:

* Increase the online visibility of Alex the Barber.
* Provide customers with information about available services.
* Display service prices clearly.
* Allow customers to submit enquiries online.
* Provide business contact information and operating hours.
* Showcase the barber's previous work through a gallery.
* Create a professional, user-friendly and accessible website.

## Website Pages

### Home

The homepage introduces Alex the Barber and provides an overview of the business, popular services and a call-to-action for customers to make an enquiry.

### About Us

The About Us page provides information about the history, mission and vision of Alex the Barber.

### Services

The Services page provides information about the different grooming services offered and their prices.

### Gallery

The Gallery page displays images representing the barber's work, services and professional environment.

### Enquiry

The Enquiry page contains a form that allows customers to submit their details and enquire about a service or appointment.

### Contact

The Contact page provides the organisation's contact information, operating hours, locations and map information.
